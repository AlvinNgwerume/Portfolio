## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the calculated authority of an institutional growth consultancy and high-end digital capital firm. It merges the austere restraint of high finance with modernist architectural precision and avant-garde editorial poise.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
Designed for enterprise executives, fund managers, visionary founders, and institutional allocators. The aesthetic instills uncompromising rigor, discreet technological sophistication, absolute operational reliability, and calculated intellectual dominance. The interface feels silent, structural, and intentional—like entering a private gallery or vault.

### Design Movement: Monolithic Minimalist Architecture & Editorial Grotesque
- **Obsidian Planes:** Tiered depths constructed from obsidian slate, deep carbon, and subtle spectral backdrops rather than standard flat surfaces.
- **Architectural Wireframes:** Hairline divisions (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), structural coordinates, dot-matrix intersections, and indexed compartmentalization.
- **Micro-Metric Typographic Rigor:** Wide-tracked uppercase metadata juxtaposed with commanding scale figures and precision monospaced markers `[001]`, `(02)`, and `SEC_04`.

## Colors

The palette revolves around deep obsidian and graphite layers illuminated by focused hits of cerulean blue and hairline silver boundaries.

### Roles and Application
- **Canvas Base (`#08090C`):** The non-reflective structural foundation. Provides the visual void for cards and content planes to float over.
- **Surface Levels (`#0F1117` to `#161922`):** Deliberate tonal steps for containers, modules, and persistent panels. Kept in low-luminance neutral ranges to eliminate visual noise.
- **Primary Accent (`#007EE6`):** Precision signaling. Reserved exclusively for interactive focus states, critical conversion anchors, metric callouts, and system-active beacons.
- **Secondary Base (`#001D3D`):** Rich midnight navy foundation used for subtle linear gradients, tinted glass surfaces, and contextual badge backgrounds.
- **Architectural Hairlines (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`): Used for structural grid divisions, cell separation, and component bounding boxes without adding physical weight.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes tension between the technical structure of **Space Grotesk**, the neutral legibility of **Inter**, and the mathematical discipline of **JetBrains Mono**.

### Structural Principles
- **Headlines & Metric Figures:** Rendered in Space Grotesk with tight negative letter-spacing for a chiseled architectural impact. Bold key metrics (`$800k+`, `+30%`) command primary focus through high visual mass.
- **Narrative Prose:** Handled by Inter to guarantee pristine readability on OLED and retina displays across long technical briefs and advisory case studies.
- **System Indexing & Metadata:** Handled by JetBrains Mono. Always used for structural coordinates (`001`, `02/INDEX`), operational status beacons, execution timestamps, and pill category codes.
- **Uppercase Badges:** Formatted in uppercase with high letter-spacing (`0.14em`) to establish crisp horizontal balance against expansive negative space.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is governed by an architectural 12-column coordinate framework where modules align directly onto hairline boundaries.

### Grid Framework & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid with a maximum content container of `1360px`, `gutter-lg` (`2rem`), and generous section margins (`margin-lg: 5rem`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout with `gutter` (`1.5rem`) and `margin-md` (`3rem`). Content modules reflow from multi-tier splits into 4-column pairing.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `margin` (`1.25rem`). Playbooks and data grids collapse strictly into single-column vertical stacks.

### Structural Rhythms
- Vertical progression leverages alternating compressed clusters (`space-xs` to `space-md` within cards) and broad structural breathing space (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl` between institutional strategic phases).
- Optional background overlay: an ultra-subtle radial dot-grid pattern (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`) spaced at 24px intervals to reinforce mathematical rigor.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through precise tonal tiers and crisp hairlines rather than heavy drop shadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Canvas Void):** `#08090C` with optional hairline grid matrix and deep ambient radial gradients (`rgba(0, 126, 230, 0.04)` at focal junctions).
2. **Level 1 (Structural Surfaces):** `#0F1117` backed by a singular hairline stroke `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Used for grid cards, modules, and list wrappers.
3. **Level 2 (Active Panels & Cards):** `#161922` with a heightened border of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)` and a directional top rim glow (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12) 0%, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02) 100%)`).
4. **Level 3 (Floating Overlays & Sticky Navbars):** Translucent glassmorphism (`rgba(15, 17, 23, 0.75)` backdrop-filter blur `16px`, bordered by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).

### Lighting & Ambient Halos
Never use murky black cast shadows. When an element requires lift, deploy a restrained, color-tinted ambient halo:
`box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 24px -4px rgba(0, 126, 230, 0.12);`

## Shapes

The design system employs a calculated duality: **chiseled architectural rectangles** for structural framework containers and **sleek pill silhouettes** for tactical interactive nodes.

### Application Rules
- **Modules, Cards, & Playbook Accordions:** Controlled structural corners (`0.5rem` to `0.75rem`), preserving rectangular integrity and alignment with the hairline grid.
- **Tactile Triggers & Badges:** Strictly pill-shaped (`9999px` / Level 3). Every button, status chip, category tag, and floating micro-element adopts the pill geometry to create immediate tactile contrast against the strict background grid.
- **Dividers & Framing Borders:** Unbroken `1px` lines maintaining crisp perpendicular intersections throughout the canvas.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Pill Silhouettes):** High-contrast interactive triggers. Filled with `#007EE6` or `#F7F8F8` (dark text `#08090C`), fully rounded pill shape (`rounded-full`), padded `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Label rendered in `body-sm` font weight 600. Includes subtle hover expansion and accent glow.
- **Secondary / Ghost Variant:** Background transparent or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`, bordered by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`. Pill silhouette. On hover: border transitions to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)` and background to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
- **Text Link with Index:** Inline links paired with monospaced directional arrows (`->`) and index markers (`[01]`). Underline is replaced with an animated hairline underline on hover.

### Badges & Category Chips
- Compact pill silhouettes (`padding: 0.25rem 0.75rem`).
- Bordered with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` over subtle fill `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`.
- Typography: `badge-label` style (uppercase, letter-spacing `0.14em`, muted silver `#8A8F9E`).
- Active or live status chips prefix an electric blue 4px circular LED node (`box-shadow: 0 0 8px #007EE6`).

### Numbered Strategic Playbook Modules (Accordions & Lists)
- Each item is delimited by a top hairline border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Layout:** Grid structure with three distinct anchors:
  1. Leading numeral: Monospaced index (`(001)`, `(002)`) in `text-muted` (`#525766`).
  2. Core narrative: Space Grotesk title with secondary body copy nested below.
  3. Trailing action indicator: Clean circular toggle (`+` / `-`) or directional micro-arrow.
- Expanded states expose darker sub-cards (`#0F1117`) containing structured deliverables and tactical KPIs.

### Metric / Stat Cards
- Enclosed inside `surface-raised` containers with hairline borders.
- Massive numerical metrics (`stat-display`) anchored at the top, colored `#F7F8F8` with deliberate accent accents (`+`, `$`, `%`) rendered in `#007EE6`.
- Micro-caption placed beneath using `index-numeral` uppercase tracking.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Dark slate fill (`#0F1117`) with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` border and `0.5rem` radius.
- Padding: `0.75rem 1rem`. Text is crisp `#F7F8F8` with placeholder text in `#525766`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#007EE6` with an immediate zero-spread ambient glow `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #007EE6, 0 0 16px rgba(0, 126, 230, 0.2)`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Minimalist geometric housings (`18px x 18px`).
- Inactive: Bordered by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` with transparent fill.
- Active: Filled with `#007EE6`, housing an ultra-fine white checkmark or inner dot.
